What is LCCC?

LCCC (Luxembourg Cards & Collectibles) is a community-driven convention dedicated to trading card games and collectibles. Whether you play Pokémon, One Piece, Magic: The Gathering or Yu-Gi-Oh!, there's a place for you here.

Discover a marketplace packed with vendors from Luxembourg, Germany and France, hunt for that missing chase card, admire ultra-rare pieces in The VAULT, and take part in pack battles, quizzes, workshops and friendly tournaments throughout the day.

Above all, LCCC is about community — bringing collectors, players and friends together to share their passion in one place.
